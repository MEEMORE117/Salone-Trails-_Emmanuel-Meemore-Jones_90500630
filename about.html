About Sierra Leone

Welcome to Salone

A country of beaches, forests and history

Sierra Leone sits on the Atlantic coast of West Africa. It is known for long white-sand beaches, rainforest wildlife, mountain views over Freetown and a deep, well-preserved history.

Salone Trails helps visitors plan trips here, with local guides, safe transport and places to stay.

Travel facts

Travel facts

The best time to visit is the dry season, from November to April. Days are sunny and the roads are easier to travel.
